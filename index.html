About Me

I received my B.S. degree from the First School of Clinical Medicine, Shanxi Medical University, Taiyuan, China, in 2019, and my M.S. degree from the Second School of Clinical Medicine, Shanxi Medical University, Taiyuan, China, in 2022. Currently, I am pursuing my Ph.D. at the School of Medicine, Southern University of Science and Technology, under the guidance of Prof. Yuliang Feng. My research broadly focuses on utilizing cross-trait genetic analysis and multi-omics integration to decipher the shared genetic architecture of complex diseases, particularly cardiovascular, metabolic, and autoimmune diseases.

Before joining SUSTech, I trained as a clinician: I am a licensed physician in Internal Medicine and completed the standardized residency training program in China. This clinical background motivates my research on translating genetic and multi-omics discoveries into precision prevention and treatment strategies.

I am actively seeking collaborators of like-minded interests. If you are interested in my work, please feel free to contact me via email: qiaojunys@163.com.

🚀 Research Thrust

"Many diseases do not occur in isolation — cardiovascular, metabolic, kidney, and autoimmune diseases share genetic roots. My goal is to decode this shared genetic architecture and translate it into precision strategies for prevention, diagnosis, and treatment."

To this end, I integrate genome-wide association studies (GWAS), Mendelian randomization, colocalization and fine-mapping, and multi-omics data (transcriptomics, proteomics, 3D genomics, and microbiome) with large-scale clinical datasets to uncover disease mechanisms and identify actionable drug targets.

Cross-Trait Genetics of Cardiometabolic Diseases

Dissecting the shared genetic architecture among cardiometabolic, respiratory, and autoimmune diseases using genome-wide cross-trait analysis. Representative work includes the shared genetic risk across major cardiovascular diseases (Nature Communications, 2025a), the causal contribution of leukocyte telomere length to CVD onset (Nature Communications, 2025b), and circulating plasma proteins as therapeutic targets for cardiometabolic diseases (Genome Biology, 2026).

Multi-Omics Integration & Precision Medicine

Building multi-omics resources and translating them into precision medicine. I co-developed EXPRESSO, a multi-omics database for multi-layered 3D genome organization (Nucleic Acids Research, 2025), and previously used multi-omics features (including transcriptomic and gut-microbiome data) to stratify patients with autoimmune diseases for precision treatment (Rheumatology, 2023a; Rheumatology, 2023b; Journal of Crohn’s and Colitis, 2023).
